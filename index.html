Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and how it works.

How do you check an influencer's engagement rate?
Traditionally, calculating an influencer's engagement rate requires manually summing up likes and comments from recent posts, dividing by their total follower count, and multiplying by 100. The Cloutlens creator audit extension automates this entire process. Simply navigate to an Instagram profile, and this Instagram influencer metrics chrome extension instantly extracts the data and calculates the true engagement rate for you.
Is Cloutlens safe and private to use?
Yes. Cloutlens is built with a strict privacy-first architecture. All calculations and data extractions occur 100% locally within your browser tab. We do not track your browsing history, we do not store creator data on external servers, and we do not sell data to third parties.
What is a good engagement rate on Instagram?
A "good" engagement rate varies by audience size. For nano-influencers (1K-10K followers), 4-5% is excellent. For micro-influencers (10K-100K), 2-3% is standard. For mega-influencers (1M+), anything above 1.5% is considered very strong. Cloutlens helps you instantly benchmark these metrics to ensure you partner with high-performing creators.
Does it work on other platforms besides Instagram?
Currently, Cloutlens is heavily optimized for Instagram creator audits. Our proprietary algorithms are specifically tuned to read and analyze Instagram's web structure for the highest accuracy. Support for TikTok and YouTube is on our roadmap.
