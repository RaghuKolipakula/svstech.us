Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

SVS Tech Extensions operate with a strict privacy-first policy:


  • Local Browser Execution: All text extraction, creator auditing, financial table parsing, and formatting occur entirely inside your browser tab.
  • Zero Data Transmission: No clipped content, financial metrics, creator stats, URLs, browsing history, or personal identifiers are ever sent to external servers.
  • No Tracking & No Ads: We do not track user behavior, use analytics trackers, or sell data to third parties.
  • Local Storage Usage: Extensions use Chrome's local storage API solely to store your personal UI preferences locally on your device.
